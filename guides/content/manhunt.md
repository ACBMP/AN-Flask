# Manhunt Guide

This is basically the script for Audi's manhunt video adjusted for a text guide format.

## Defense

* Your goal on the stunning round is to wall up, not "hide and survive". This means sticking
with teammates in a wall spot you can't get Arielled from to start what we call a stun lock.
* Basically when a pursuer is stunned by your teammate and you're mashing B, you can
stun them before they can kill you as they get up. So if you both keep doing this, you're
gonna rack up a stupid amount of points.

## Offense
* Your goal as a pursuer is to 1. get silent kills, at least 5 cus of the 750 OP kill streak. 2.
Don't let this happen to you and your teammates. (also if you dont have any teammates
around, hit the y out button to respawn).
* So first how to get silent kills. Well obviously don't let your meter drop below silent but
what's not obvious is unlike the sequel games which start your meter just on the top of
discrete, meaning youre literally forced to walk everytime time you're in line of sight,
Brotherhood starts it at the top and not only that, if you learn to hold the fast walk button
down while stop and starting to run repeatedly, you can basically run around without
dropping below silent. Now this does take a little practice, but it is truly a gamechanger
* So now we know that, how do we stop stun locks and still get silent kills? Well first off
don't get caught in it, dont shoot, discrete or chase kill cus you're just throwing points
away. Instead keep a safe enough distance from their abilities and wait for the perfect
moment to go in. Its also worth baiting targets out of abilities by bluffing when you're
going in till they're defenseless. You can also play mind games this way. You can also
bait them to run at you with mute towards a spot where you can run up a wall and aerial
them from.

## Ability sets

We also have a [separate page on ability sets for manhunt](/guides/manhunt-sets).
The following explains the abilities and what they're used for.

### Offense

* Long lasting knives: You can use this to bait abilities, stop them from running once they have no abilities, use
it to get free aerial kills or prevent them from grouping with their teammates
* Rapid Reload Disguise: Specifically rapid cus for some reason if you activate it whilst being in low profile for the
first 2 seconds, that is walking, not sprinting or climbing, you can get a 200 point bonus
for it.
* Slow Acting Poison: For the love of god use this sparingly. You only want to try using this if your target is busy
stunning your teammate or if you're being sneaky as its often more of a hindrance. Also
your teammates can still kill it, so be careful who you use it around.

##### Perks

* Resistance to make it harder to be stun locked
* Silent Hunt to make it far easier to control the silent meter, and thus move faster

##### Streaks

* 750 point kill streak
* Reset Cooldowns or Score x2

### Defense

* Long lasting smoke bomb: You knew this was coming but here's 7 things you probably didn't know.
1. Smoke bombs looks tall, but they're actually flat, so if your pursuer is more than an
inch above you then it wont effect them.
2. Slow acting smoke is the best for manhunt as you can potentially stun 2 pursuers with
it
3. Theres a delay when you throw smoke bombs, this often means feeling like you've
been killed through it, just learn to use it earlier.
4. Due to this delay, don't bother using it aggressively.
5. Smoke bombs are even more delayed for teammates, the reason is because unlike
you, they can't initially dodge a pursuer in your smoke
6. Press smoke right before you press stun and it will protect you whilst you're in
animation. A typical move is to mute stun and then throw the smoke right before
pressing stun.
7. You can actually bait targets by pretending to walk into their smoke when you're
actually just outside of it pressing the kill button.
* Long lasting or Strong Mute: This stops your pursuer from killing or using abilities but unlike smoke, you can use this
aggressively. Long lasting is better for chasing your pursuer down for longer and Strong
is more forgiving.
* Strong charge: When in animation you cant be killed, you'll accelerate faster than normal throwing
pursuers off and the stun range will max out so its pretty effective , however keep in
mind if your pursuer dodges it, you could become very vulnerable, you also cant throw a
smoke when getting a stun from it, again leaving you vulnerable.

##### Perks

* Overall cooldowns
* Wallrunner
* Blender

##### Streaks

* Kill streak doesn't apply
* Reset Cooldowns, so after your third death, you'll always spawn in with abilities again.

## Advanced tips

1. Drop Stuns - The 360 no scope of ACB. Basically your pursuer cant kill you for a split
second after landing leap from a platform like a barrel or a ledge, use that to get a stun.
You'll look like a boss and its useful when you have no abilities.
2. Bumps - When you bump a civilian or player, the animation and position is often not
synchronized to where other players see you, it often results in them zooming at you on
your screen or you zooming at them on their screen.
3. Learn to Unlock - It only takes one of their teammates to run at you and get a free
stun. Sometimes not locking at all can be safer as you can get kill anyone in range.
4. Delay pursuers - In the same way you bait targets out of abilities, you can also waste
pursuers time by pretending to run at them like you're gonna use abilities.
5. Cut offs, once you know the wall spots, you can anticipate where your targets are
headed. Use this to surprise attack them or just to stop them from regrouping
6. Stun then Run - Now running from your pursuers all day might stop them from getting
points, but it also stops you and your teammates from getting points, since you can't
stunlock and the other team is then just going to do the same back to you anyway.
Players will leave and start their own private lobby. So trust me as someone who's been
playing for over a decade, get at least 1 stun before you run away, even if it means
dying, its better for you in the long run.
7. Protect teammates - Usually when its your turn to stun the pursuer on the ground,
your teammate is on guard duty, however if they dont have any abilities, you can still use
your abilities if they stay close.
8. Make sure you ALL get a 750 streak. If you're playing super competitively then this is
a must.
9. Stealth - It can be pretty mediocre in this mode, however you can still use some
sneaky tricks such as entering a blend group round the corner of the wall spot, arriving at
the wall spot before the target does or even just flank them by going the opposite side of
where your teammates are coming from, just don't try to walk out in the open slowly.
10. On defense, keep track of your opponents' progress on streaks. You want to minimize the total number of streaks your opponents get. In an average competitive lobby, players tend to die between 6 and 8 deaths, so you can roughly assume one +750 kill streak will be achieved per player. The in-game log tells you which player a teammate has stunned, so this can be used to identify which player is the one you should ideally die to.
