### Spawn logic

Spawns are dependent on enemy and teammate locations, with priority on spawn close to teammates but far away from enemies.
When two spawns are similar enough, the game chooses the spawn with a lower number. You can see the numbering system on [our map spawn pages](/guides/#maps).

For the player hosting the lobby, the game ignores enemy locations, meaning the player can even spawn directly on an enemy.
As a result, it can be especially helpful to coordinate spawns on the team with the host.

We have a [separate page with a detailed description of the spawn algorithm](spawns) for those interested.

### Kill priority

When two players are trying to kill the same target or one player is trying to kill someone who's also trying to kill someone, the game prioritizes them the following way:

1. Aerial kills (from roofs)
2. Jump kills (player jumped from another area onto the ground next to their target)
3. Normal ground kills
